Story & Company

Why a rhino

Rhino Republic is the umbrella brand universe for all of our alcohol brands. The mascot is the Indian one-horned rhino: a single horn, plated skin, and a calm refusal to step off the path.

Character over prettiness

Our range is planned as a premium one, for drinkers who want character and resilience rather than decoration. Every spirit gets its own name; all of them share one mark.

Armour

The one-horned rhino’s plated skin stands for confidence and resilience. An Indian species, with one horn only.

Republic

A place you belong to. Drinkers, bartenders and makers are all citizens of the Republic.

One mark

One recognisable rhino, used with restraint. Character over decoration, on every bottle we make.
