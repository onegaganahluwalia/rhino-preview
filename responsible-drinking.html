Responsible drinking

Drink responsibly

Consumption of alcohol is injurious to health. Be safe, don’t drink and drive.

Our commitments

  • Legal age. Our website and our communication are for adults of legal drinking age only. In Maharashtra this is 25. Other states set different ages; always follow the law where you are.
  • Never for minors. We do not use youth imagery or cartoon characters, and we ask everyone never to supply or serve alcohol to anyone under the legal age.
  • No drinking and driving. If you drink, don’t drive. Plan a ride home before you go out.
  • No online sales. We do not sell alcohol online.
  • No claims. We do not suggest that alcohol improves health, performance, confidence or social success.

This website is for brand information only. It is not an offer of alcohol, and it is not directed at anyone below legal drinking age.

Looking after yourself and others

  • Know your limits and pace yourself.
  • Don’t drink if you are pregnant, on medication that interacts with alcohol, or about to operate machinery. If unsure, ask a doctor.
  • Respect anyone who chooses not to drink.

Support

If you or someone you know needs support, these official services are a starting point: Tele-MANAS, the Government of India’s National Tele Mental Health Programme, on 14416 or 1800-891-4416 (telemanas.mohfw.gov.in). For a current official drug de-addiction helpline, see the numbers published by the National Institute of Social Defence, Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment (nisd.gov.in). These services are run by the Government of India, not by us.
