Privacy notice

Your privacy on this website

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This notice explains what personal data this website handles, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). The short version: we do not collect personal data through this website.

Who we are

This website is run by Rhino Spirits & Beverages Private Limited (CIN U11011MR2026PTC482018), registered office Lodha Amara W15-2803, Casa Sereno D, Kolshet Road, Thane (W) 400607, Maharashtra, India. For any personal data we do handle, we are the Data Fiduciary.

What we collect

  • No forms, no accounts, no sign-ups. There is nothing on this site where you type in personal details.
  • No cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. We do not use Google Analytics, social media pixels or any similar tool.
  • Age confirmation. When you confirm you are of legal drinking age, your browser stores one small value (rr_age_ok) in its local storage so you are not asked again. It stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.
  • Server logs. Like any website, the hosting provider’s servers may automatically record technical details of each request, such as IP address, browser type and time, for security and to keep the site running. We do not use these logs to identify or profile visitors.

If you email us

If you write to onerhinorepublic@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and to handle your enquiry, and we keep it only for as long as that needs. We do not sell or share it for marketing.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may ask us to tell you what personal data of yours we hold, to correct or erase it, and to withdraw any consent you gave. You may also nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf. Email us at onerhinorepublic@gmail.com with the subject line “Privacy request” and we will respond.

Grievances

For any concern about how we handle personal data, contact us at onerhinorepublic@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Age

This website is about alcoholic beverages and is meant only for adults of legal drinking age in their state (25 or older in Maharashtra). We do not knowingly handle personal data of children.

No online sales

Rhino Republic does not sell alcohol online. There is no shop, cart or payment on this website.

Changes

If this notice changes, we will update it on this page and change the date above.
