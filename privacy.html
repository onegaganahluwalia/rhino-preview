Privacy notice

Your privacy

Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Draft: pending approval

This notice explains what personal data Rhino Spirits & Beverages Private Limited handles through this website, and how you can exercise your rights. We aim to meet the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025. Most of those duties apply from 13 May 2027; we will follow this notice from the day the site is published.

This website does not sell anything and does not use advertising or analytics cookies. We do handle some personal data, as set out below: if you email us or, once they are connected, if you use a contact or trade form; and technical logs that our host may record to run and secure the site.

Who we are

Rhino Spirits & Beverages Private Limited (CIN U11011MR2026PTC482018) is the Data Fiduciary for personal data handled through this website. Registered office: Thane, Maharashtra, India. Contact: charge@rhinospiritsandbeverages.com. For questions about this notice or about our processing of your personal data, write to privacy@rhinospiritsandbeverages.com with the subject “Privacy request”, addressed to Gagandeep Singh Ahluwalia, Director.

No cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking

We do not set cookies and we do not use Google Analytics, social media pixels, advertising or similar tracking tools. Fonts and images are served from this website only. If that changes, this notice will be updated before any such tool is used.

Age confirmation

This website is for adults of legal drinking age in the State or Union Territory where they are (25 or older in Maharashtra). If you select “Yes, I am of legal drinking age”, your browser stores one value on your device only (rr_age_ok), containing a flag and an expiry, for 30 days, so you are not asked again. We do not receive that value, and we do not use it to identify you. If you select “No”, nothing is stored. You can delete a stored value by clearing this site’s data in your browser. This is a self-declaration. We do not independently verify your age.

Contact and trade forms

Today these forms are a preview only. Submitting them does not send or store what you type. Once a form service is connected, the following will apply, and we will name the processor in this notice before that happens.

  • What we will collect. From the contact form: your name, email address, topic and message. From the trade enquiry form: your name, company, work email, type of licensed trade, State(s) of operation and message. We will also record that you gave consent, and the date and time. Please do not send ID documents, government ID numbers, licence numbers, passwords, payment details or health information. If an optional phone field is added to either form, that number will be used only for the same purpose.
  • Purpose. We will use these details only to read, reply to and handle your enquiry (including, for trade enquiries, to assess a possible licensed-trade relationship). We will not use them to send marketing, and we will not sell them.
  • Legal basis. Your consent, which you give by ticking the consent box and submitting the form (DPDP Act s. 6). You may withdraw consent at any time by emailing privacy@rhinospiritsandbeverages.com; withdrawal will be as easy as giving consent. Withdrawal does not affect handling already done.
  • Who will process it. We are the Data Fiduciary. The form service Form service TBC will process the submission on our behalf as a Data Processor, under a contract that requires reasonable security safeguards. We will also use our website host, Host TBC, to operate the site. We will not share your personal data with anyone else except a processor acting on our instructions, or if the law requires us to.
  • Retention. We will keep an enquiry for 24 months after our last correspondence on it, or earlier if you withdraw consent or ask us to erase it, unless a longer period is required to comply with law (for example if the enquiry becomes a contract, a tax record or a legal claim). Technical logs of the processing may need to be kept for at least one year once Rule 8(3) of the DPDP Rules, 2025 applies (from 13 May 2027).
  • Until the forms are connected, please use charge@rhinospiritsandbeverages.com instead. Email enquiries are handled as set out below.

Server logs and hosting

The website is planned to be hosted in India by Host TBC. The host, acting as our Data Processor, may automatically record technical details of each request (such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and time) to operate, secure and diagnose the site. We do not use these logs to profile visitors or for advertising. We keep them only as long as needed for those purposes, and for any longer minimum that the law then requires (see Retention above). We will not transfer personal data outside India unless we first meet any requirement specified by the Central Government (DPDP Act s. 16). If the host or the hosting location changes, this notice will be updated.

If you email us

If you email charge@rhinospiritsandbeverages.com or privacy@rhinospiritsandbeverages.com, we receive your email address, any name or signature you include, and the content of your message. We use this only to reply and handle your enquiry or request, including a privacy request. We keep it for 24 months after our last correspondence, or earlier if you withdraw consent or ask us to erase it, unless a longer period is required to comply with law. We do not sell it or use it for marketing. Please do not send ID documents, government ID numbers, passwords, payment details or health information unless we have specifically asked for a document needed to complete a request.

Your rights

You may, by emailing privacy@rhinospiritsandbeverages.com with the subject “Privacy request”:

  1. ask for a summary of the personal data of yours we are processing and of our processing activities, and the identities of any Data Fiduciaries or Data Processors with whom it has been shared (DPDP Act s. 11);
  2. ask us to correct, complete or update inaccurate or misleading personal data, or to erase it (s. 12);
  3. withdraw consent where consent is our basis for processing (s. 6(4));
  4. use the grievance process below (s. 13); and
  5. nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become unable to exercise them (s. 14).

We will need enough information to identify your request. We will respond within a reasonable period, and not later than 90 days from receipt. These rights apply in full from 13 May 2027; we will honour valid requests from the day this site is published.

Grievances

Write to Gagandeep Singh Ahluwalia, Director at privacy@rhinospiritsandbeverages.com with the subject “Privacy grievance”. We will acknowledge your grievance and respond within a reasonable period, and not later than 90 days from receipt. If you are not satisfied with our response, or if the period has passed, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India. Under the DPDP Act you should use this company’s grievance process first (s. 13(3)). Please use the official channel published by the Board or by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology when you complain.

Age and children

This website is only for adults of legal drinking age in the State or Union Territory where they are (25 or older in Maharashtra). Other States and Union Territories set their own ages; always follow the law where you are. For the DPDP Act, a child is a person who has not completed 18 years. We do not knowingly process personal data of children. We do not track or behaviourally monitor children, and we do not target advertising at anyone, including children. If we learn that we have received personal data of a child, we will erase it. The age gate is a self-declaration and does not verify age or parental relationship.

Security

We will take reasonable security safeguards to protect personal data in our possession or under our control, including data processed on our behalf, to prevent a personal data breach (DPDP Act s. 8(5)). If a personal data breach occurs, we will intimate the Data Protection Board and each affected Data Principal as required (s. 8(6); Rule 7, from 13 May 2027).

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update this page and the date above. If the change affects personal data we already hold, we will also inform the people then affected, where that is practical.
