About

One republic. Every spirit we make.

Rhino Republic is the umbrella brand universe for all of our alcohol brands. Each spirit carries its own name and character, and every one of them belongs to the Republic.

Our range is planned as a premium one, built for drinkers who want character and resilience rather than prettiness. The mascot is the Indian one-horned rhino: a single horn, plated skin, and a calm refusal to step off the path.
